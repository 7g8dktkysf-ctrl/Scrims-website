PLAY FAIR

SCRIM RULES

Every team is expected to follow these rules throughout the session.

RULE 01

No Hacks or Cheats

Any use of hacks, cheats, scripts, exploits or unauthorized third-party software is strictly prohibited.

RULE 02

Player POV Recording

All participating players must record their POV throughout the scrim. POV recordings may be requested for verification or dispute review.

RULE 03

Self-Aid & Emergency Pickup

Players should follow the designated self-aid and emergency-pickup procedure communicated by the organizers whenever required.

RULE 04

Fair Play

Teams must compete fairly and respect all other participating players and organizers.

RULE 05

Team Information

Teams must submit accurate player and captain information before participating.

RULE 06

Organizer Decision

Match-related disputes and rule violations will be reviewed by the organizers based on available evidence.
